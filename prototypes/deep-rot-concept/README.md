# Deep Rot — concept prototype (dive-and-sanity)

**Hypothesis:** If the player dives floor by floor while Sanity drains faster with depth,
the "deeper or climb up?" decision will feel tense. We will know if, over 8+ dives, the
player pushes past their last safely-banked depth in at least 1/3 of dives and keeps
playing ~15 minutes without being asked.

**Riskiest assumption:** the decision gets solved ("always climb at X Sanity").

**Run:** open `prototype.html` in any browser (double-click). No install.
Controls: WASD/arrows move · hold Space near a creature to grab · E at the rope (climb) or hole (deeper).

**Measure:** the surface screen logs every dive (deepest floor, outcome, Sanity left,
banked/lost, "pushed past last safe depth"). Use **Copy log** after a session.
